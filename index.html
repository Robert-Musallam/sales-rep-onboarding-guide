Manager's guide

Onboarding a new sales rep — what's on you

Once you register a new hire, most of the setup takes care of itself. This is the short version of what happens, and the handful of things only you can do.

01

Your role, start to finish

You're the owner of this process for your new hire. Everything below is on you — the system handles the rest automatically once you do your part.

  • 1 Submit the Onboarding Registration Form for your new hire, picking your own name from the manager dropdown.
  • 2 Make sure your new hire completes the Basic Information form. Nothing else moves forward until they do.
  • 3 Check off each box on the Onboarding Checklist — Gusto, Business License, Welcome message — the moment it's actually true. Checking a box is what kicks off everything tied to it.
  • 4 That's it. Account creation, team chats, and the company-wide welcome all happen on their own once you check the box.

There's no deadline on any of this — one step simply waits for the one before it. Nothing happens until you or your new hire complete the step that triggers it.

04

The checklist boxes — what each one does

These three don't depend on each other — check them in whatever order makes sense, whenever each one is actually true.

Gusto
Creates their company account — email and Teams login, ready for them to use.
Business License
Gets them plugged into the team: a 1:1 welcome chat with you, their region's "Phone Room" group with their supervisor, and their region's existing sales team chat — plus a reminder to have Teams and WhatsApp set up on their phone.
Welcome message
Posts the public welcome announcement for the whole company, and sends them the official welcome email with their first-week setup steps.
