📘 What Academy is

Academy teaches you everything you need to sell and design for Rock N Block: our products, how to measure and price a job, how to handle objections, and how to close. It works on your phone or computer, and you can go at your own pace within the timeline below.

🧭 How the training is set up

There are 7 modules, and they open one at a time. Each one unlocks after you pass the one before it.

  1. Foundations: who we are, how we work, and the basics of the job.
  2. Products & Selling Points: our turf and hardscape products, and why customers choose them.
  3. Measurement & Pricing: how to measure a yard and build a price. This module includes a diagram exercise.
  4. Objection Handling: how to answer the concerns customers raise most often.
  5. Closing & Follow-up: how to ask for the sale and follow up the right way.
  6. Simulator: practice sales conversations with an AI customer at three levels: warm, standard and hard.
  7. Field: real-world training with your manager, including a ride-along, a Day 5 evaluation and Housecall Pro setup.

✅ How you pass each module

  • Modules 1 to 5: each lesson ends with a short check. You need 90% to unlock the next lesson, and you can retake it as many times as you need. When all the lessons are done, you take the module exam (also 90%) and write a short explanation in your own words.
  • Module 6: pass a simulated conversation at each of the three levels.
  • Module 7: complete the ride-along, the Day 5 evaluation and your Housecall Pro setup. Your manager signs off on these.

🎯 What we expect from you

  • Log in regularly. Aim for steady progress, ideally a little every day. If you don't log in for 7 days, your manager is notified.
  • Learn it, don't rush it. The 90% pass mark is there because you'll use this material in front of customers. Read the lessons carefully and look at the photos and product specs.
  • Do your own work. The written answers and simulator conversations show us how you think and talk to customers.
  • Ask for help early. If something isn't clear, reach out to your manager. We'd rather answer a question today than see you stuck next week.

👀 Your manager can see your progress

Your manager sees which module and lesson you're on and when you were last active. This isn't about watching over you. It's so we can help at the right moment.

We're excited to have you on the team. Take your time, ask questions, and get ready to be one of our best!
