Manager's guide

Onboarding a new sales rep — what's on you

Once you register a new hire, most of the setup takes care of itself. This is the short version of what happens, and the handful of things only you can do.

01

Your role, start to finish

You're the owner of this process for your new hire. Everything below is on you — the system handles the rest automatically once you do your part.

  • 1 Register your new hire — in the Onboarding app (New Rep) or with the classic Registration Form link. Pick your own name from the manager dropdown. They immediately get a text with their info form.
  • 2 Make sure your new hire completes the Basic Information form. Nothing else moves forward until they do.
  • 3 Check off the boxes on their checklist the moment each one is actually true — Gusto signed, license assigned, welcome sent. Checking a box is what fires everything tied to it.
  • 4 That's it. Account creation, team chats, the company-wide welcome, their Housecall Pro user, and the training test all run on their own from there.

There's no deadline on any of this — one step simply waits for the one before it. Nothing happens until you or your new hire complete the step that triggers it.

04

The checklist boxes — what each one does

The boxes don't depend on each other — check each one whenever it's actually true.

Gusto
Check it once the rep is added in Gusto and their contract is signed. Creates their Microsoft account — company email and Teams login, with a temporary password saved on their card in the app.
Microsoft license assigned
Check it once you've assigned their Microsoft 365 license. Gets them plugged into the team: a 1:1 welcome chat, their "Phone Room" group with the phone-room team and regional supervisor, their region's sales chat — plus a text with the Teams and WhatsApp download links for their phone.
Welcome message
Posts the public welcome announcement for the whole company, and sends the official welcome email from the onboarding mailbox to their new address (copy to their personal email).
Also on the checklist
Business license — record-keeping only, no automation. · Housecall Pro user — checking it creates their HCP employee in the right regional office automatically. · GreenSky — the rep submits their own approval from their personal onboarding page. · Business cards — checking it emails the order to the business-card contact. · Training — completes itself when the rep passes the readiness test on their onboarding page.
